# Project Schedule and Sprint Plan

Team 032 – Himalayan Coders | Problem Statement 2: Student Mentoring and Career Support Platform

## 1. Scheduling tool

The team uses **Jira** (project IITM BS SE PROJECT, key KAN) for scheduling and task tracking.

- **Epics** group the work of each milestone or sprint (KAN-7 to KAN-11).
- **Tasks** carry one owner, a start date and a due date, and move across the board columns To Do, In Progress, In Review and Done.
- The **Timeline** view of Jira is used as the Gantt chart. The **Board** view is the Kanban board.
- Each task records its owner, so the board also shows each member's contribution.

## 6. Iterations and meetings

- Each sprint is split into weekly iterations.
- Scrum meetings are held twice a week, led by the Scrum Master. Each meeting covers what was done, what is planned next and blockers.
- Each sprint ends with a user feedback session, and the feedback shapes the next sprint plan.
- Tasks are reviewed by the Code Reviewers and tested by the Testers before they move to Done.
