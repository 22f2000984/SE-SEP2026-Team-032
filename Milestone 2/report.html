3. Design of Components

The platform follows a client-mediated model. Students and mentors each maintain their own profile, but they cannot browse each other. The client (coordinator) reviews both sides, proposes a match, and tracks it until it ends.

ComponentResponsibilities
Authentication and rolesRegistration, login, and three roles: student, mentor and administrator. Every action is checked against the role on the server, because hiding a link in the interface is not security.
Student moduleMaintain goals, skills, preferences and availability. View the assigned mentor after the match is confirmed. Give a readiness response, feedback, and raise concerns.
Mentor moduleMaintain qualifications, expertise, bio and availability slots. Accept or decline match requests, and see accepted mentees.
Administrator moduleVerify mentor profiles, propose matches, change match status, review concerns, and see delayed proposals and an overview by status.
Match workflowControls the status of a match: proposed, confirmed, active, completed or cancelled. Only allowed transitions are accepted and each change is logged.
NotificationsTells the people involved when a match is proposed, accepted, declined or changes status.
Data layerA relational database holding users, profiles, availability, matches, status log, feedback, concerns and notifications.

Match status flow: proposed → confirmed (mentor accepts) → active → completed. A proposed or confirmed or active match can be cancelled. A declined proposal becomes cancelled, and the coordinator can then propose a new match.

5. Database Design

TablePurpose and key constraints
USEROne row per account. Email is unique. Role is student, mentor or admin.
STUDENT_PROFILEGoals, skills, preferences and readiness. One per student user (user_id unique).
MENTOR_PROFILEQualifications, expertise and verification status. One per mentor user.
AVAILABILITY_SLOTWeekly time slots of a mentor. Many slots per mentor.
MATCHLinks a student and a mentor. Records the administrator who proposed it and its status.
MATCH_STATUS_LOGHistory of every status change, with who changed it and when.
FEEDBACKRating and comments about a match.
CONCERNA concern raised about a match, open until the coordinator resolves it.
NOTIFICATIONMessages sent to users, optionally linked to a match.

6. Frontend Pages and Screenshots

The frontend is a set of 11 linked pages in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It runs from the file index.html without installing anything. Sample data is kept in the browser so the whole flow can be tried: the coordinator proposes a match, the mentor accepts, and only then does the student see the mentor. There is no backend yet; the API is built in Milestones 3 and 4.

8. Open Questions and Risks

  • Whether a mentor may decline a match, and what happens to the student after a decline (the prototype returns the student to the coordinator).
  • Which channels are used for notifications, and whether email is needed.
  • How long student and mentor data is kept, and who may see feedback.
  • Time zone handling for availability slots.
